- prospective revocation of new creation, new public release, and new redistribution when covered material is intentionally withdrawn from every official source location; and
- recipient-specific 90-day removal on notice, including derivatives under that recipient's control and no republication.

## Short identifier

Use `OpenPersona-1.0` as the short identifier.

## What the license can cover

A subject can be one subject or many. It can be a real person, fictional character, animal, creature, avatar, mascot, persona, entity, or other subject.
