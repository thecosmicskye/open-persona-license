# Applying the Open Persona License v1.0

1. Add `LICENSE.md` to the repository, release, or source package.
2. Add a subject record that identifies the subject or subjects, approved names, attribution name, official source locations, official notice channels, and covered material.
3. Put covered material in the locations identified by the subject record.
4. State that the covered material is available under Open Persona License v1.0.

Recommended short identifier: `OpenPersona-1.0`.

The license text and documentation may be reused under CC0 1.0 Universal. Covered material is not CC0 unless the licensor separately says so.
